All access. Can change site settings, manage admins, assign moderators, edit members, factions, content, XP reviews and staff notes.

All website tools: manage members and factions, edit content, review Premium and community XP requests, and assign moderator scopes. Cannot change the protected Owner or promote another Administrator.
