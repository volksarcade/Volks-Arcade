VOLKS ARCADE NETWORK · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

What information the community site uses, why it uses it, and how you can manage it.

LAST UPDATED · 16 OCTOBER 2026

Who operates VOLKS ARCADE

VOLKS ARCADE is a gaming and creator community operated by its owner. For privacy questions or requests, email volksarcade@gmail.com.

Information we handle

  • Account and sign-in: email address, chosen display name, and authentication details managed by Supabase Auth. If you use Google sign-in, Google shares basic account information such as your email address and profile name.
  • Profile information: biography, favourite games, social links, avatar, banner, privacy choices, and any game character, faction, or rank claim you submit.
  • Community activity: platform chat messages, message edits and pins, staff applications, faction and supporter requests, and moderation records needed to run the community safely.
  • Progress and purchases: challenge progress, XP, VA Coin balances, reward ownership, and transaction records for in-site XP exchanges and cosmetic purchases. These balances are private to your account.
  • Basic technical data: sign-in session and site preferences stored by your browser, plus technical logs held by our hosting and service providers.

Chat and notification retention

Platform chat messages and account notifications are automatically and permanently deleted once they are more than 30 days old. A scheduled database job runs nightly. This retention rule does not delete account or profile data, XP, VA Coins, rewards, faction memberships, or moderation records.

How we use it

We use this information to create and secure accounts, show profiles according to your privacy settings, operate chat and community features, review membership and moderation requests, maintain XP and VA Coin records, and respond to support or privacy requests. We do not sell personal information.

Public profiles, chat, and staff access

If you make your profile public, the profile details you choose to share can be viewed by visitors. Do not add information you want to keep private to a public bio or social link. Platform chat is visible to members with access to that room and may be reviewed by authorised moderators and administrators. Staff can see account and request details needed for their assigned duties.

Service providers and embedded content

VOLKS ARCADE uses Supabase for account authentication and database services, GitHub Pages for website hosting, and Google when you choose Google sign-in. Pages may embed videos or content from YouTube and TikTok. Those services may receive technical information from your browser when their content loads and handle it under their own privacy policies. PayPal handles its own checkout if you choose a PayPal subscription or donation; VOLKS ARCADE does not receive your card or bank details through this site.

How long information is kept

Account and community records are kept while they are needed to operate the service. You can ask us to correct or delete your account information using the contact details below. Some records, such as staff moderation decisions or transaction history, may be retained for a limited period when needed to protect the community, resolve disputes, or keep accurate account balances. Service providers may retain encrypted backup copies for their normal backup period.

Your choices and requests

You can choose whether your profile is public and edit the profile information you provide in the site. You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. We may need to verify that the request is yours before acting on it. You can also revoke Google access through your Google Account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, account-data requests, or to report a concern, contact volksarcade@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current community website. Features and service providers may change; this page will be updated when our data practices materially change.
