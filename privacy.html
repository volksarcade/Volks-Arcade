VOLKS ARCADE NETWORK · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

What information the community site uses, why it uses it, and how you can manage it.

LAST UPDATED · 2 NOVEMBER 2026

Who operates VOLKS ARCADE

VOLKS ARCADE operates this gaming and creator community. For privacy questions or requests, email volksarcade@gmail.com.

Information we handle

  • Account and sign-in: email address, chosen display name, and authentication details managed by Supabase Auth. If you use Google sign-in, Google shares basic account information such as your email address and profile name.
  • Profile information: biography, favourite games, social links, avatar, banner, privacy choices, and any game character, faction, or rank claim you submit.
  • Community activity: platform chat messages, message edits and pins, staff applications, faction and supporter requests, and moderation records needed to run the community safely.
  • Progress and purchases: XP, VA Coin balances, reward ownership, and transaction records. A VA Coin order can include a PayPal order or capture reference, selected pack, amount, currency, payment status, and the account ID needed to credit coins. Wallet balances are private to your account; we do not store card or bank details.
  • Basic technical data: sign-in session and site preferences stored by your browser, plus service and security logs held by our providers. Depending on the service, logs can include your IP address, request time, browser/device information, and approximate region.

Chat and notification retention

Platform chat messages and account notifications are automatically and permanently deleted once they are more than 30 days old. A scheduled database job runs nightly. This retention rule does not delete account or profile data, XP, VA Coins, rewards, faction memberships, or moderation records.

How we use it

We use this information to provide and secure accounts, show profiles according to your privacy settings, operate chat and community features, review membership and moderation requests, maintain XP and VA Coin records, and respond to support or privacy requests. Depending on the feature, our basis is providing the service you request, your choices or consent for optional profile information, our legitimate interests in security and community moderation, or a legal obligation where one applies. We do not sell personal information.

Public profiles, chat, and staff access

If you make your profile public, the profile details you choose to share can be viewed by visitors. Do not add information you want to keep private to a public bio or social link. Platform chat is visible to members with access to that room and may be reviewed by authorised moderators and administrators. Staff can see account and request details needed for their assigned duties.

Service providers and embedded content

VOLKS ARCADE uses Supabase for account authentication and database services, GitHub Pages for website hosting, and Google when you choose Google sign-in. Pages may embed videos or content from YouTube and TikTok; those services may receive technical information from your browser when embedded content loads. PayPal handles checkout for VA Coin packs and any PayPal subscription or donation you choose. VA Coin checkout is currently in PayPal Sandbox test mode, not real-money mode. PayPal and other providers handle data under their own terms and privacy notices. Some providers may process data outside the UK; their documentation explains their transfer arrangements. VOLKS ARCADE does not store your card or bank details.

How long information is kept

The automatic 30-day deletion applies to platform chat messages and account notifications only. Accounts, profiles, XP, VA Coins, purchases, faction memberships, and moderation records are not deleted by that scheduled job. We keep these records while needed to operate and protect the service, handle account balances or resolve disputes, and meet legal record-keeping duties where applicable. You can ask us to correct or delete account information; some records may need to be retained for the reasons above. Hosting and service providers apply their own operational and backup retention periods.

Your choices and requests

You can choose whether your profile is public and edit the profile information you provide. You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to ask about restriction, objection, or portability rights where they apply. If you have given consent for an optional feature, you can withdraw it for future use. We may need to verify that a request is yours before acting. You can revoke Google access through your Google Account settings. If you are in the UK and are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Contact

For privacy questions, account-data requests, or to report a concern, contact volksarcade@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current community website. Features and service providers may change; this page will be updated when our data practices materially change.
